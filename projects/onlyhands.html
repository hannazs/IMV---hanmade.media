OnlyHands

“OnlyHands” is a short comedy/satire film about a girl who becomes increasingly obsessed with hands. What starts as a slightly odd fascination slowly escalates, until the film reveals itself as a fake advertisement for OnlyHands, a fictional platform. During the making of this film, I was responsible for audio, and helped out with the conceptualisation. In post-production, I handled the rolor correction and grading, as well as final tweaks in the edit.
