East? West? Berlin.

“East? West? Berlin” is a short documentary I made during my studies as part of a group of five. The film explores the differences between East and West Berlin and how their histories still shape the city today. Working on this project was a lot of fun, and it’s one I really loved making. Within the group, I took on several roles, including filming, recording sound, and putting everything together in the final edit, with color correction and grading.
