When Life Keeps Blossoming

“When Life Keeps on Blossoming” is a short documentary made during my studies as part of a group of five. The film follows Marianne, who has gradually lost her eyesight due to retinitis pigmentosa, and shows how she has learned to live with and adapt to this change. I recorded and edited the audio for the project and also supported the team in the final cut.
