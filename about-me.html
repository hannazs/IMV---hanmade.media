About Me

Hi, I’m Hanna. I studied Multimedia Production and love working creatively across different formats — filming, photography, audio, editing, illustration, and design. I enjoy shaping ideas and emotions through tools like Premiere Pro, Audition, Steinberg Nuendo, DaVinci Resolve, Blender, After Effects, Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop, Figma, and Procreate. I’m curious by nature and like experimenting with different ways of telling stories, whether visually, through sound, or on the page. Outside of my work, I love watching movies, listening to music, and spending time with my two cats, Misty and Toufas, and my two bunnies, Bean and Toffee.
